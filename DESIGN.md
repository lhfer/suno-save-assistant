# Visual direction

## Thesis
Warm paper, orange sound, quiet confidence. The product should feel like a well-made local utility for people who make music. Extend the existing extension palette and rounded controls.

## Content
Lead with the product and one visible save interaction. Follow with three concise benefits, transparent verification, and a simple install sequence. Technical constraints belong in the FAQ and installation guide.

## Colors
- Paper: #F7F5F1
- Ink: #302B27
- Muted ink: #726357
- Action orange: #C95732
- Verified green: #4F7A60
- Rules: #DDD4C8

## Type
System sans and PingFang SC carry Chinese UI and body copy. Georgia is reserved for the English product name and large editorial figures, connecting music and a personal archive. Use tabular figures for progress and timing.

## Interaction
1. A restrained waveform responds only while a simulated save is active.
2. Link, queue, validation and file states transition in place.
3. Recovery and deduplication can be explored explicitly, without real requests or downloads.
Honor reduced motion and pause animation when the page is hidden. Keep controls keyboard accessible.

## Avoid
No neon, gradient type, generic feature-card mosaics, fake testimonials, autoplay sound, fabricated live recordings, or personal account/song data. The demo must identify itself as simulated. Product UI panels are allowed because they are the working surface being demonstrated.
